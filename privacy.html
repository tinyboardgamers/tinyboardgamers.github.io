Privacy Policy

App Name: Quoridor P2P • Effective Date: May 20, 2024

1. Overview

Tiny Board Gamers built the Quoridor P2P app as a free tactical board game. This SERVICE is provided by Tiny Board Gamers at no cost and is intended for use as is.

2. Information Collection and Use

Our app does not collect, store, share, or process any personal information, location data, or analytics. All game sessions occur peer-to-peer locally between devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

3. Network Permissions

The app requests Wi-Fi and network state permissions solely to facilitate peer-to-peer discovery and gameplay between nearby devices. No data is transmitted to external servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@tinyboard.games.
